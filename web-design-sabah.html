How much does a website cost in Sabah?

It depends on what you need, because every project is different. A simple one-page site costs far less than a large multi-page site with a blog and custom features. Rather than guess, tell us your goals and we will send a clear, fixed quote up front, with no hidden fees and no lock-in.

A standard business website takes 2–4 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on how quickly we receive your content and feedback. Larger sites with e-commerce or many pages take 4–8 weeks.

Yes. We work with clients across Sabah - Kota Kinabalu, Sandakan, Tawau, Penampang and beyond - and remotely with Malaysian and Australian businesses. Everything can be handled over WhatsApp, email and video calls.
