FAQ

Common questions

Which payment methods can you support?

We integrate Malaysian-friendly payment gateways including FPX online banking, e-wallets (Touch ’n Go, GrabPay), credit/debit cards via iPay88 or Stripe, and cash-on-delivery where it suits your business.

How much does an online store cost?

E-commerce sites start from around RM6,000 depending on product count, payment and delivery integrations. We scope it precisely and give you a fixed quote.

Can customers in Sabah and West Malaysia both order?

Yes. We set up shipping zones, rates and couriers (J&T, Pos Laju, etc.) so you can sell across Sabah, all of Malaysia, or internationally.

Will my products show up on Google?

Yes — we add product schema and optimise your category and product pages so they’re eligible for rich results and Google Shopping, and can rank organically.
