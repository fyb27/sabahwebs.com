FAQ

Common questions

Why Webflow instead of WordPress?

Webflow has no plugins to update or get hacked, loads faster out of the box, and gives you a clean visual CMS your team can actually use. For most brochure and content sites it means lower maintenance, better security and better Core Web Vitals than a typical WordPress build.

Can I edit a Webflow site myself?

Yes. Webflow’s Editor lets you update text, images, blog posts and projects directly on the page, with no risk of breaking the design. We train you, or manage it for you.

Is Webflow good for SEO?

Very. Webflow outputs clean, fast, server-rendered HTML with full control over titles, meta, schema and URLs - everything Google and AI search need. We build every Webflow site SEO-first.

Do I have to host with Webflow?

Not necessarily. Webflow hosting is excellent and simple, but we can also export and self-host your site (as we do for our own) if you want maximum control over cost and infrastructure.
