FAQ

Common questions

How much does SEO cost in Malaysia?

Honest SEO for a Sabah SMB typically runs RM800–RM2,500 per month depending on how competitive your market is and how fast you want results. We explain the full breakdown in our guide on how much SEO costs in Malaysia.

How long does SEO take to work?

Most businesses see meaningful movement in 3–6 months, with stronger results from 6–12 months as content and authority compound. Anyone promising page one in two weeks is not being honest — we explain why in our blog.

Do you do local SEO and Google Business Profile?

Yes. Local SEO is the fastest win for most Sabah businesses. We optimise and grow your Google Business Profile, build local citations, fix your NAP consistency, and target “near me” and city-level searches like “… in Kota Kinabalu”.

Can you help me get found by AI like ChatGPT?

Yes — it is called GEO (Generative Engine Optimization). We structure your content and entity signals so AI assistants cite your business. Read our guide to AI SEO in Malaysia to see how.
