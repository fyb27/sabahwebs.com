Co-Founders, SabahWebs

We’re Bing and Liang - two partners and the people behind SabahWebs. We started this studio for one simple reason: too many brilliant Sabah businesses are let down by slow, outdated websites, or have no website at all while their competitors get found on Google every day.

We build websites the way they should be built - fast, mobile-first, secure, and search-optimised from the very first line of code. No bloated WordPress plugins, no mystery quotes, and no jargon. Just honest work that turns your website into your hardest-working salesperson.

Between us we’ve worked with real Sabah and Malaysian businesses across trades, tourism, healthcare and retail - from scaffolding specialists in Kota Kinabalu to eco-tourism in the interior. Whatever your industry, the goal is the same: more of the right customers finding you, trusting you, and getting in touch.
